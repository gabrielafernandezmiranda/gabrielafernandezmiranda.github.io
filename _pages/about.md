I am a Ph.D. candidate in Psychology and Neuroscience at Duke University, working with 
[Dr. Felipe De Brigard](https://www.imclab.org/people){:target="_blank"}. 
My main research interest is the relationship between forgiveness, memory, and emotion. 
Specifically, I focus on how forgiveness impacts the experience of remembering past wrongdoings. 
I aim to understand how reappraising these memories allows people to recall them less painfully,
and how this process is related to forgiveness. More generally, I am interested in understanding 
how people can effectively regulate emotional aspects of their memories to overcome 
traumatic violent episodes. As part of this, a key area of my research is conducted 
in Colombia with victims of the internal armed conflict.



I earned her B.A. in Psychology from Universidad Javeriana and my M.A. from Universidad de los Andes (Bogotá, Colombia).
