For the Fall of 2026, I am serving as a graduate teaching assistant for [Dr. Cristina Salvador's](https://sites.duke.edu/culturelab/bios/) 
Cultural Psychology course. My primary goal is to lead engaging discussion sections that explore questions 
related to the course material and deepen students' understanding of research in cultural psychology. 
Through these discussions, I seek to foster critical thinking about the consequences of overlooking 
cultural differences in psychological research and the importance of developing more inclusive approaches. 
In addition to grading exams, I review students' biweekly reflection blogs, in which they connect course topics 
to their own lives.
